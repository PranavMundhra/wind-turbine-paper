## Defaults until the team says otherwise
- "We" for our own work; past tense for what was done, present for what results show.
- One claim per sentence. Numbers carry units and the run they came from.
- No banned filler (see the skill's drafting guide). No adjectives that the evidence can't back ("robust", "novel", "significant" without a test).
